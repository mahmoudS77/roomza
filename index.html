Roomza

Simple File Sharing Between Nearby Devices

Roomza is an Android application that makes it easy to share photos, videos, audio files, documents, applications and messages between nearby devices.

How Roomza Works

Create a room and invite nearby people using a QR code or invitation code. Members can share and download files within the room.

Roomza is designed to make nearby file sharing simple, convenient and accessible.

Privacy Policy

Roomza respects your privacy. The application does not sell your personal information to third parties.

Files shared through Roomza are transferred between participating devices according to the sharing session created by the users.

Roomza may request permissions required for features such as connecting to nearby devices and accessing files selected by the user.

We only use information necessary to provide the application's features and functionality.
